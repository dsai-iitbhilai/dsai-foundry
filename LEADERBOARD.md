## 📜 How It Works

1. **Submit** your work via a pull request (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md))
2. **Get reviewed** — a maintainer reviews your submission
3. **Get merged** — your entry joins the showcase and you appear on the leaderboard
4. **Earn badges** — hit milestones and get recognized
5. **Get featured** — outstanding entries are selected for the monthly spotlight
